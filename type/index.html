Body text

For the last eleven years I’ve worked at the seam where product strategy meets craft — deciding not just how a thing should look, but whether it should exist, who it’s for, and what it makes possible. Most of that has been at Microsoft: reshaping how people sign in across Windows, how millions meet Edge for the first time, and how a single consent can span an entire account.

I care about the unglamorous parts — the empty states, the error someone hits at 2am, the one sentence in a dialog that decides whether they trust you. Good design, to me, is the work that disappears: it feels inevitable, and it holds up whether you glance at it for a second or study it for an hour.
